Everything in the app

One-tap logging
Income, Expense or Transfer commits instantly and the screen stays open. Fields reset, wallet and currency stay put.
Tags, not categories

FAQ

Questions people ask before they switch.

Everything about accounts, privacy, offline use and currencies — answered before you download.

Does Wumbi need an account or a bank connection?
No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password, and Wumbi never links to your bank. You open the app and start logging — nothing to create, nothing to connect.
Where is my financial data stored?
On your phone only, in an AES-256 encrypted database whose key lives in the device secure storage. There is no Wumbi server and no cloud copy, so your transactions never leave the device. One tap resets everything.
Does Wumbi work offline?
Yes, fully. Exchange rates are cached on-device for 12 hours and every transaction stores the rate it was created with, so your history never shifts when rates change later.
Which currencies does Wumbi support?
13 currencies including Bitcoin to 8 decimals. Each wallet keeps its own currency, you can type an amount in any currency, and your total is converted to the one you choose.
How is Wumbi different from a category-based budgeting app?
You type free-form #tags inline instead of maintaining a category tree, and a transaction commits with one tap — about three seconds, with the screen staying open for the next one. A tag map then shows which tags move money together.
When does Wumbi launch and on which platforms?
Wumbi is coming to iOS and Android. Join the waitlist and you get one email on launch day — no newsletter, no spam.
Which languages does Wumbi speak?
Seven: English, French, German, Dutch, Turkish, Russian and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout for Arabic.
